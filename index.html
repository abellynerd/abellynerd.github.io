I am a second-year math student at the University of Virginia, interested in spectral theory and graph theory. I aspire to pursue graduate studies in mathematics, with the ultimate goal of becoming a professor. I also major in music, with a concentration in composition.

I love puzzles. Crosswords, sudoku, Rubik’s cubes, you name it; I’m addicted to the epiphany of deciphering a punny clue, or twisting the last algorithm to line up the colors. This feeling is what draws me to study mathematics. Whether I’m figuring out the best approach to a real-world problem, coming up with a research methodology, or writing a proof, math presents a fantastic puzzle.

Outside of mathematics, I enjoy playing trumpet for the Cavalier Marching Band, and I am a member of YoungLife and Club Swim. Some of my hobbies include cooking, building LEGO, hiking, and mountain biking. In addition to my native English, je parle français és magyarul beszélek!
